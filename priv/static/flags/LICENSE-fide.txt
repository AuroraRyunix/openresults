This is the word "FIDE" in lettering, not FIDE's logo. The logo (the
knight) is not freely licensed - Wikipedia holds it as a non-free file
for its own article on FIDE and nowhere else - so it is not shipped
here. "FIDE" is the name and a trademark of the Federation
Internationale des Echecs; it appears only to say which federation a
player is listed under, and implies no endorsement of this software.
